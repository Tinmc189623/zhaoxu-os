# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Zhaoxu OS to be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy project.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that builds the project:

- Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback about code, boot logs, and
  design decisions.
- Focusing on what is best for the operating system and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Asking before rewriting published history, changing version numbers, or
  landing a design that the project has not agreed on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical address
  or email address, without explicit permission.
- Pushing malicious code, hiding a self-developed component as an upstream
  port, or knowingly shipping a boot or installer change that destroys a
  tester's disk without warning.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, commit
messages, chat channels, and any other space where someone is representing the
project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the
project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to
**admin@nexsteaduser.com**. Include what happened, where, when, and any
supporting links. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially
as possible. Maintainers will remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not
follow this code, and will explain moderation decisions when it is safe to do
so.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use these steps in proportion to the severity and repetition of an
incident:

1. **Correction.** A private or public note explaining the problem and asking
   for a change in behaviour, with an apology where appropriate.
2. **Warning.** A warning that continued behaviour will lead to a temporary
   ban, including a defined period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A defined period during which the person may not
   interact with the project, its contributors, or its spaces.
4. **Permanent ban.** Removal from the project for a pattern of violations,
   harassment, or aggression.

Maintainers who are involved in an incident must not decide on its handling.
An alternative maintainer, or the project owner if none is available, handles
that report.
